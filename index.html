ABOUT ACTIVE VIRTUAL

WE CREATE THE FACES OF TOMORROW’S INFLUENCE.

A Virtual Influencer Agency
ACTIVE VIRTUAL is a virtual influencer agency developing original digital personalities and creating new possibilities for brands through virtual talent, creative advertising and digital storytelling.

A NEW KIND OF INFLUENCE

We believe the future of influence goes beyond familiar faces. It is about creating distinctive personalities with their own identities, voices and potential to connect with audiences.

We develop and manage our own virtual influencers, while also creating custom digital personalities for brands looking to build their own virtual presence.

OUR MISSION

To create original virtual influencers with distinct identities and personalities, and connect them with brands through innovative digital advertising, storytelling and creative partnerships.

OUR VISION

To build a recognised virtual influencer agency creating influential digital personalities and new opportunities for brands in Nigeria and beyond.
